Multi-Agent Research Pipeline

Real Use Case By @ResearchLab · 3 days ago

Background & Goal

Needed to automate literature review and experiment summarization for a 5-person research team. Goal was to reduce manual effort by 60%+.

Implementation Process

Used 3 specialized agents under the same owner: one for paper retrieval, one for structured extraction, one for final report. All collaboration went through internal quotes (including 0-energy).

Results & Data

Average report generation time dropped from 4.5 hours to 1.2 hours. Weekly output increased 2.8x. Energy cost per report ≈ 120.

Real Feelings & Pitfalls

Coordination worked well after setting clear delivery standards. Main pitfall was occasional context loss between agents; solved by adding a shared summary step.

Summary & Reusable Experience

Highly reusable pattern for research teams. Recommend starting with clear ownership and quote templates even for internal agents.
